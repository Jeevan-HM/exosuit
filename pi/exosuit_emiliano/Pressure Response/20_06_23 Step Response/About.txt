About: Pressure Response Data
June 2020

Pressure Response, inflation / deflation of actuators
IAC=composite Actuator. IFB = Inflatable Beam actuator

Data Files:

Composite (IAC) Actuator Tests:

	- data_step_HighPressure.txt: preliminary pressure response with IAC (composite) and with a high pressure source inlet. The code used valves to shut inlet air. (June 23)
	- data_def_1port.txt: pressure deflation response with 1 outlet port. The inlet pressure was the same as the actuator settle pressure (no high pressure source). (June 24)
	- data_def_2port.txt: pressure deflation response with 2 outlet port. The inlet pressure was the same as the actuator settle pressure (no high pressure source). (June 24)
	- data_inf_1port.txt: Inflation response, with high source pressure. Using 1 port for air inlet. (June 25)
	- data_inf_2port.txt: Inflation response, with high source pressure. Using 2 individual ports for air inlet. (June 26)
	- data_def_vacuum.txt: pressure deflation response with 1 vacuum deflation port. The inlet pressure was the same as the actuator settle pressure (no high pressure source). (July 9)



Inflatable Beam Actuator Tests:
	- data_IFB_1port.txt: 1 outlet port for each. Inlet pressure same as actuator settle pressure.(June 24)
	- data_IFB_HighPressure.txt: The inlet source pressure was set at a very high pressure. The code used valves to shut inlet air. (June 24)
