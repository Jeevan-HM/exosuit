About: step_response 20_09_16		September 16, 2020

This folder contains the codes and data utilized for unit step response tests of inflation and deflation of the HIP IAC (composite).

1st column on .txtx data file is time [sec]. 2nd column is pressure [psi]


INFLATION:
Source pressure at 60 psi. Desired pressure to reach is 30 psi. 8mm diameter tubing was used; tubing length from inflation valve to actuator was varied.
Data Files for Inflation:
	- data_inf_1.txt --> data_inf_5.txt = inflation trials 1-5. 1 meter long tube.
	- data_inf2m_1.txt --> data_inf2m_5.txt = 5 inflation trials using 2 meter long tubing from the valve to the actuator.


DEFLATION:
Source and set pressure = 30 psi. 8mm diameter tubing was used. Tubing length from deflation valve to actuator was varied.
Data Files for Deflation: 
	- data_def_1.txt --> data_def_5.txt= no vacuum was used. 1 meter long tube. 5 trials
	- data_vac1m_1.txt --> data_vac1m_5.txt = vacuum was used. 1 meter long tube. 5 trials
	- data_vac2m_1.txt --> data_vac2m_5.txt = vacuum was used. 2 meter long tube. 5 trials
