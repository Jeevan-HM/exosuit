About: data_10_01		October 1, 2020

This folder contains the smart shoe data for walking on the threadmill using the hip with one IAC and the knee with 2 IAC. 2 vacuums were connected in parallel to serve as a single exhaust.



DATA FILES BELOW:

1st Condition Tested: Each actuator was connected one port directly to exhaust and the other port directly to inlet air. The pressure sensor was conected to the inlet air tubing with a "T" connector. Pressurized to 20 psi, with a source pressure of 40 psi
Data Files 1st Condition:
	- data_10_01_a.txt =  Walking at 0.5 m/s speed (1.8 k/h on the threadmill)
	- data_10_01_b.txt = Walking at 0.33 m/s speed (1.2 k/h " ")



2nd Condition Tested: The same changes in Condition #1 were maintained and the following were added: The valves for exhaust to vacuum were mounted on the human (at the waist using a velcro belt) to reduce the tubing length between the valves and the actuators.
Data Files for 2nd condition:
	- data_10_01_c.txt = walking at 0.5 m/s speed
	- data_10_01_d.txt = walking at 0.33 m/s speed


3rd Condition Tested: The vacuums valves for the hip and knee were separated so that each exosuit joint has its own vacuum. The black vacuum was set for the knee exosuit, and the blue vacuum was set for the hip exosuit. This festo valves were attached to the human. This configuration seemed to yield the fastest deflation.
Data files for 3rd condition:
	- data_10_01_e.txt = walking at 0.5 m/s speed
	- data_10_01_f.txt = walking at 0.33 m/s speed
